What is a Scrub Diaspora Credit Report?+

A lender-grade credit profile that consolidates a Nigerian applicant's BVN-linked credit history, active facilities, repayment behavior and an affordability assessment into a single verifiable Scrub Score — built specifically for verifying applicants living abroad.

Who is it for?+

Banks, mortgage lenders, microfinance and fintech underwriters who need to verify and assess Nigerian applicants in the diaspora before extending credit, a facility, or a back-home mortgage.

How does Scrub verify a diaspora applicant?+

We match BVN and government identity, pull licensed credit-bureau data, and layer our localized fraud models on top — so you get an identity-verified, fraud-screened report rather than self-reported figures.

How current is the data?+

Reports are generated on demand and reflect the latest bureau and facility data available, with active balances and repayment status refreshed at pull time.

Can I get reports via API?+

Yes. Everything available in the dashboard is available through the Scrub API, so you can request a Diaspora Credit Report inside your own underwriting flow.
